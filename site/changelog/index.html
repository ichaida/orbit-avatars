v0.2.0October 17, 2026

A better fit. A few little extras.

Four original accessories join the crew, glasses get a more considered fit, and the gallery becomes a space to make something yours.

New

  • Headphones, a flower, a bow, and a star pin. Each can be worn over a hijab and paired with glasses.
  • A redesigned studio with a larger preview, grouped controls, live React code, and a quick path from the collection to your editor.
  • This changelog, so library and artwork changes have a home.

Fixed

  • Round and square glasses now follow the eye positions of each face. Hijab frames are narrower and sit inside the exposed face instead of drifting onto the scarf.
  • Glasses on uncovered faces now center on both eyes, with a connected bridge.

For developers

The accessory option now also accepts headphones, flower, bow, and star. Head accessories replace hats and companions. Crowns still exclude glasses; the new additions allow them.

New option values can change the appearance of seed-only avatars between library versions. Pin the package version or persist complete traits for a selected portrait.

Meet the crew.

The first public release: original SVG portraits, a dependency-free TypeScript core, and a gallery to explore them.

  • Seeded faces, hair, skin tones, outfits, glasses, and little companions.
  • Three hijab silhouettes with independent scarf colors and exclusive crown options.
